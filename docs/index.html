Executive Snapshot

Product

Built for daily follow-up, not just record keeping.

Work2U is designed to help users act faster, keep context together, and reduce the mental load of remembering every next step.

  • survey-first onboarding
  • CRM, tasks, clients, and cases
  • internal calendar and reminders
Pricing

Affordable entry point, with room to scale.

The pricing logic is built around affordability first, so freelancers and small teams can start without heavy monthly pressure.

  • Starter for solo users
  • Elite for growing teams
  • Enterprise for custom workflows
Launch

Clear execution path for production rollout.

The docs set a practical order for schema, calendar, email, billing, and launch ops so the team can move safely.

  • Supabase execution checklist
  • internal calendar companion
  • final launch and webhook notes
Work2U keeps the core workflow calm and practical: survey first, login next, then a dashboard that adapts to how the user actually works.
Survey-first onboarding Internal calendar first Malaysia + global billing AI assist, not AI overload

Execution Roadmap

Now

Core workflow and internal calendar

Focus on survey onboarding, CRM core, email sending, reminders, and the internal calendar layer that makes follow-up easier.

Next

Billing, invoices, and automation

Strengthen the money flow with invoices, receipts, document sharing, payment reminders, and a cleaner automation layer.

Later

Deeper AI and connector expansion

Expand AI assistance, optional Google connectors, and higher-tier controls only after the core experience stays stable.

Receipt Flow

Upload

Save receipts the moment they happen.

Users can upload receipt images or PDFs from mobile or desktop so the system becomes the main place to keep expense proof.

  • camera capture or file upload
  • store by date and month
  • attach to expense or vendor
Categorize

AI reads the receipt automatically.

OCR and AI can extract vendor, date, amount, and receipt text so the user spends less time typing and correcting data.

  • vendor and date capture
  • amount and tax detection
  • suggested category tags
Close Month

See monthly summary and profit clearly.

Work2U can roll up revenue, expenses, and overhead into a clean monthly view, making P&L and planning easier.

  • monthly expense summary
  • revenue minus expenses minus overhead
  • simple P&L view

Monthly Expense Dashboard

Overview

Track spend, overhead, and receipts together.

A monthly dashboard gives users a single view of expense totals, receipt count, and the categories that matter most.

  • monthly totals
  • receipt count
  • category breakdown
AI Assist

Let AI flag missing data or unusual spend.

AI can help identify missing vendor names, suspicious totals, or uncategorized receipts before month-end close.

  • missing field alerts
  • duplicate detection
  • unusual spend flagging
P&L

Move from dashboard into profit view.

The dashboard feeds a cleaner profit report so revenue, expenses, and overhead can be reviewed together.

  • gross revenue
  • monthly expenses
  • net profit view
