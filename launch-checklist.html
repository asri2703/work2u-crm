Work2U Launch Checklist

Use this page to do the final review before you let user sign up, pay, and onboard.

This page is designed as the practical final stop before launch. Check legal pages, email login readiness, billing, webhook routing, and entitlement sync together. For demo and sales, show the survey-first flow, seeded workspace, and 7-day trial path.

Work2U is built to keep business follow-up, communication, billing, and AI assistance in one place. Before launch, make sure the public pages are live, email login works cleanly, and billing events update access correctly. For the demo story, take a prospect from survey, to dashboard recommendation, to starter pack, to billing.

1. Legal And Public Pages

Public pages

Privacy Policy and Service Policy should be reachable from the landing page and the app sidebar.

Contact

Make sure the support email and contact channels are live before public release.

2. Email Login Readiness

  • Email login works without Google dependency.
  • Magic link or password flow has been tested.
  • Resend sender and verification path are ready.
  • Google connector stays optional for V1.

3. Billing

Malaysia

Billplz collection ID, secret key, and X Signature key should be ready before live checkout.

Global

Stripe publishable key, secret key, and webhook secret should be ready for the global route. The checkout should start with a 7-day trial, require a card, and allow the user to continue or unsubscribe later from the portal.

4. Webhooks

  • Stripe endpoint: /api/billing/webhook
  • Billplz callback: /api/billing/billplz/callback
  • Billplz redirect: /api/billing/billplz/redirect
  • WhatsApp webhook: /api/whatsapp/webhook

5. Product Flow

  • Survey before login works.
  • Login route works for email first.
  • Overview, setup, billing, and reports pages load without error.
  • Payment success updates subscription and entitlement state.
  • Trial users can continue or unsubscribe from billing settings without needing support to cancel manually.
