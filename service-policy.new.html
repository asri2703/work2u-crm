Work2U Service Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Draft for review. This page supports product clarity, billing expectations, and Google OAuth policy readiness. Please have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.

This service policy explains how Work2U is provided, what you agree to when you use it, and the rules around plans, billing, automation, and connected services. The goal is to keep the experience clear, useful, and fair.

1. Service Scope

Work2U provides CRM, task management, communication, accounting, reporting, automation, and AI assistant features. Some features may require third-party integrations or plan upgrades.

2. Accounts and Workspaces

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of your workspace and profile information.
  • You are responsible for access to your connected email, Google, WhatsApp, Telegram, and billing accounts.
  • Super Admin, Admin, and User roles may have different permissions.

3. Acceptable Use

  • Do not use Work2U for illegal, harmful, deceptive, or abusive activities.
  • Do not attempt unauthorized access to accounts, data, or infrastructure.
  • Do not use automation to spam, impersonate, or violate messaging platform rules.
  • Do not upload malicious content or interfere with service operation.

4. Billing and Plans

  • Starter, Elite, and Enterprise plans may have different limits and features.
  • Billing may be handled by Billplz for Malaysia and Stripe for global users.
  • Payment confirmation, webhook events, and entitlement sync determine active access.
  • Failed or reversed payments may suspend or reduce access.

5. Refunds and Cancellations

Refunds, if any, depend on the payment provider, your subscription terms, and the specific plan or arrangement you purchased. Enterprise plans may include custom terms in a separate agreement.

6. AI Features

  • AI suggestions are generated to assist you, not replace your judgment.
  • You remain responsible for content reviewed and sent through Work2U.
  • AI usage may be limited by quota, plan, or connected AI account settings.

7. Third-Party Integrations

Features that rely on Google, Supabase, Resend, Billplz, Stripe, WhatsApp, Telegram, Cloudflare, or other providers are also subject to those providers' terms and availability.

8. Service Availability

We aim to keep Work2U available and reliable, but downtime, rate limits, API changes, or third-party interruptions can happen. We may perform maintenance or update features from time to time.

9. Suspension and Termination

We may suspend or terminate access if you violate this policy, if required by law, or if continued access risks the service, other users, or our partners.

10. Support

Support may be provided by plan tier, email, or other channels we publish. Enterprise support can include separate onboarding or SLA terms.

11. Changes

We may update this service policy as Work2U evolves. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated policy.
