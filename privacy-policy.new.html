Work2U Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Draft for review. This page supports Google OAuth consent screen readiness and user transparency. Please have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.

Work2U ("we", "our", or "us") helps you keep business work in one place, from CRM and tasks to accounting, communication, automation, and AI assistance. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data such as name, email, login provider, and workspace details.
  • Business data such as leads, clients, tasks, cases, services, invoices, receipts, notes, and reports.
  • Communication data such as message content, delivery status, channel metadata, and contact identifiers.
  • Usage data such as login timestamps, feature usage, plan status, and audit logs.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and error logs.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide and operate the Work2U platform.
  • To authenticate users and manage workspaces.
  • To send reminders, invoices, receipts, and workflow notifications.
  • To power AI suggestions, drafts, and workflow assistance.
  • To enforce plan limits, billing status, and entitlement access.
  • To improve reliability, security, and product quality.

3. Third-Party Services

Work2U may connect to third-party services depending on your setup and plan, including Supabase, Google, Resend, Billplz, Stripe, Cloudflare, Telegram, and WhatsApp-related connectors.

These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy policies. We only share the data needed to provide the connected feature you enabled.

4. AI Features

If you enable AI features, prompts, drafts, summaries, or workflow instructions may be sent to the AI provider you connected or the AI service configured for your workspace.

We recommend that you avoid sending highly sensitive data unless it is necessary for the workflow and you understand the provider settings you are using.

5. Data Retention

We keep workspace data as long as your account or workspace remains active, or as needed to provide billing, audit, and support functions. You may request export or deletion subject to legal and operational requirements.

6. Security

We use access controls, environment-based secrets, and server-side validation for sensitive operations. No system is perfectly secure, but we aim to protect your data with reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

7. Your Choices

  • You may choose which integrations to connect.
  • You may disable certain automation or AI features.
  • You may request access, export, or deletion where applicable.
  • You may contact us if you need help managing your account data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and provide notice where appropriate.
