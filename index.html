FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Work2U CRM?
Work2U is an all-in-one business management platform that combines tasks, leads, clients, accounting, AI assistance, and communication tools into one beautifully designed system.
How is my data stored?
Your data is stored locally in your browser with the option to sync to cloud storage. Enterprise customers can use their own database.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes! Pro and Enterprise plans support custom domains. You can host Work2U on your own domain like crm.yourcompany.com.
Does it integrate with WhatsApp?
Yes! Work2U includes WhatsApp Web integration. Generate a QR code, scan with your phone, and chat with clients directly from the dashboard.
Is there an AI assistant?
Yes. Work2U includes AI integration with multiple providers including Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, and OpenAI ChatGPT. Switch between them as needed.
Can I export my data?
Absolutely. Export invoices as PDF, reports as CSV, and full backups anytime. Your data is always yours.
How do I get support?
Email us at enquiry@work2u.io or WhatsApp +60 13 773 2703. Pro customers get priority support within 24 hours.
