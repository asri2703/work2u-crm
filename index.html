FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Work2U CRM?
Work2U is an all-in-one business management platform that combines tasks, leads, clients, accounting, receipt keeping, AI assistance, and communication tools into one beautifully designed system.
Which plan should I choose?
The survey recommends the best fit based on your role, team size, channels, and automation needs. Starter is for solo users, Elite is the best-value team plan, and Enterprise is for custom workflow and governance.
Is there a free plan?
Pricing starts from RM39 per month. Founding users get free onboarding and priority setup during launch, but the product itself is not a free plan.
How is my data stored?
Core data lives in Supabase with row-level security. The browser only handles the UI session, and enterprise customers can keep a more custom data setup.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes. Custom domain and branded rollout support is available on higher plans, especially for enterprise setups that need a more controlled experience.
Does it integrate with WhatsApp?
Yes. Work2U supports a QR-based WhatsApp Web style connection so you can message clients directly from the dashboard without forcing the WhatsApp API path for v1.
Is there an AI assistant?
Yes. AI helps draft replies, suggest next steps, and support workflow planning. You can also connect your own AI account if you want more control over usage.
Can I export my data?
Absolutely. Export invoices as PDF, reports as CSV, and backups anytime. Your data stays under your workspace ownership model.
Can I upload receipts?
Yes. Users can upload receipt images or PDFs so Work2U can keep expense records organised, make monthly expense review easier, and support cleaner P&L reporting.
How do I get support?
Email us at enquiry@work2u.io. Starter gets standard support, Elite gets priority support, and Enterprise gets dedicated onboarding and SLA-style support.
