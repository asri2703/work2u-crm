FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Work2U CRM?
Work2U is an all-in-one business management platform combining tasks, leads, clients, accounting, receipt keeping, AI assistance, and communication tools in one system.
Which plan should I choose?
The registration flow recommends a fit based on your role, team size, channels, and automation needs. Starter is for solo users, Elite is the best-value team plan, and Enterprise covers custom workflow and governance.
Is there a free plan?
Pricing starts at RM39 per month. Founding users get free onboarding and priority setup during launch, but the product itself is not a free plan.
How is my data stored?
Core data lives in Supabase with row-level security. The browser only handles the UI session, and enterprise customers can arrange a more custom data setup.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes. Custom domain and branded rollout support is available on higher plans, especially for enterprise setups that need a controlled experience.
Does it integrate with WhatsApp?
Yes. Work2U supports a QR-based WhatsApp Web style connection, so you can message clients from the dashboard without requiring the WhatsApp API for v1.
Is there an AI assistant?
Yes. AI helps draft replies, suggest next steps, and support workflow planning. You can also connect your own AI account for more control over usage.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Export invoices as PDF, reports as CSV, and backups at any time. Your data stays under your workspace ownership model.
Can I upload receipts?
Yes. Upload receipt images or PDFs so Work2U keeps expense records organised, making monthly review easier and P&L reporting cleaner.
How do I get support?
Email enquiry@work2u.io. Starter gets standard support, Elite gets priority support, and Enterprise gets dedicated onboarding and SLA-style support.
Why does the trial need a card?
The 7-day Stripe trial uses a card so you can continue smoothly after testing, while still being able to cancel before renewal if the plan is not the right fit.
